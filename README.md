# Hilt Helper

Plan your next Old School RuneScape Combat Achievements from **Easy through Grandmaster**. Hilt Helper uses your in-game progress, account type, and current preparation to build a route toward Ghommal’s hilts 1–6.

## Get started

1. Install **Hilt Helper** from RuneLite’s Plugin Hub.

2. Log in on a standard world and open **Hilt Helper** in the sidebar.

3. Follow **Next up**. Open the task for its requirements, walkthrough, and upcoming objectives.

The full route and reward claims require membership. [Plugin Hub listing](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/ironman-hilt-guide)

## Prepare for your tier

Open **Preparation → Tier preparation** to see the unfinished route options for your current milestone in one place.

- **Quests & levels** lists the quests and skill levels to complete or confirm, with the activities they unlock.

- **Items & supplies** groups gear, consumables, and spell supplies by activity. It shows shortages, restocks, ready kit, and checks you can confirm in game.

- **Access & setup** includes activity unlocks, world changes, party requirements, and checks such as a grown Hespori patch.

Open your personal bank when prompted. The guide uses observed inventory, equipment, bank supplies, and supported rune pouch contents. Ultimate Ironmen use carried supplies, and Group Ironman supplies count when held personally. Use **Confirmed available** after checking a requirement in game.

## Browse the checklist

The **Checklist** starts with your suggested tasks in route order, then shows the remaining achievements. All **655 tasks** have encounter portraits. Use search, **Hide completed**, or the Activity, Tier, Achievement type, and Task status filters to narrow the list.

Task cards include **Requirements & kit** and **Walkthrough & sources**. Use **Show task on screen** to pin a compact objective while you play. Verified completions advance the route and clear the completed task note.

## Track milestones and rewards

**Reward milestones** shows your target and claim status for all six hilts. Game-verified achievements earn their rank’s points across tiers. Grandmaster tracks the required task IDs and verified Group Ironman roster exemptions.

To check a reward in RuneScape, open **Character Summary**, select **Combat Tasks Completed**, and then open **Rewards**. Ghommal and Laidee Gnonock are outside the Warriors’ Guild.

## Set route preferences

Hilt Helper supports mains, Ironman, Hardcore, Ultimate, and Group Ironman account types. In **Account & backups → Route preferences**, choose solo-only suggestions, defer full raids, or opt in to Wilderness activities.

Confirm your party size from the relevant task card when everyone is ready. Use **Task options** to defer a task or follow an activity, and **Skipped tasks & activities** to restore deferred choices.

## Manage progress and get help

**Account & backups** includes import and export, import preview and undo, saved-versus-game reconciliation, and preparation corrections. It also includes **Read Me**, **Report a Bug**, and optional Hespori reminders when available.

Progress is stored locally per account. The guide reads RuneLite’s game observations to select routes and check preparation. Portrait and walkthrough sources are credited with the plugin.
