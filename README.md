# Hilt Helper

Hilt Helper tracks your Old School RuneScape Combat Achievements in RuneLite. It suggests solo or group routes to Ghommal’s hilts 2–4 using your completed tasks, remaining points, and account unlocks.

| Guide | Reward | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Medium | Ghommal’s hilt 2 | 169 |
| Hard | Ghommal’s hilt 3 | 436 |
| Elite | Ghommal’s hilt 4 | 1,100 |

The Elite guide in 0.6.2 covers all 166 Elite-ranked tasks and can recommend unfinished achievements from other tiers. [0.6.2 release notes](https://github.com/Zipdude/hilt-helper/releases/tag/v0.6.2)

## Install

Open **Plugin Hub** in RuneLite, search for **Hilt Helper**, and select **Install**. Log in on a standard world and open **Hilt Helper** from the sidebar. The full routes and rewards require membership. [Plugin Hub listing](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/ironman-hilt-guide)

## Plan and complete tasks

1. Choose **Medium**, **Hard**, or **Elite**. Use **Change guide** to switch goals. Progress, skips, and preparation are shared across guides.
2. Choose **Solo** for independent activities or **With a group** to include party activities. Open **Route & requirements** to compare unlocked and locked points for both routes by task type. Elite gives practice-heavy tasks a later place in the route and offers solo alternatives while you wait for a party.
3. Open the suggested task or search the checklist by task name, rule, boss, or in-game ID. **Requirements & kit** shows what to bring and which unlocks you need. For Elite party tasks that ask for a team size, use **Confirm party size…** there once your team is ready. Open **Walkthrough & sources** for attempt tips and links to fuller guides.
4. Complete the task in game. The sidebar updates your points and advances to the next unfinished objective. At the verified target, it shows how to claim the selected reward at the Warriors’ Guild.

The guide supports mains, regular and Hardcore Ironmen, Ultimate Ironmen, and ranked, unranked, and Hardcore Group Ironmen. Item guidance reflects what the account can use or obtain. Open **Task options** to skip a task or activity and allow the route to find replacements. **Skipped tasks & activities** lets you restore them. Wilderness tasks enter the route only when you opt in, and the guide shows relevant account-mode risks.

## Checklist and preparation

| Control | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Suggested tasks** | The current plan toward your remaining points, including reserves when useful. |
| **Selected tasks** | The guide’s core objectives. |
| **Reserve tasks** | Extra tasks you can swap into your plan. |
| **All guide tasks** | The guide’s full checklist. Use **Filters** to choose an activity or hide completed tasks. |
| **Missing requirements** (Elite) or **Blocked tasks** (Medium and Hard) | Tasks that still need an item, quest, level, or access unlock. |
| **Required & missing items** | Required gear and supplies. **Show all missing items** groups shortages, checks you can confirm, and saved confirmations. |

Open your bank when prompted. The guide checks observed personal bank, inventory, equipment, and held rune pouch contents. Ultimate Ironmen use carried and equipped supplies. Group players get credit for supplies in their own containers. A saved bank scan can prompt you to reopen the bank if the guide cannot verify a combined quantity. Common tradeable supplies for mains appear as restock instructions when appropriate.

The guide checks quests, skill levels, diary progress, and boss access where RuneLite provides that information. Spell checks use spellbook unlocks, Magic level, and available runes, with a reminder to switch spellbooks if needed. Task cards show practice tips, party coordination, and private-instance setup where relevant.

The task screen keeps **Back to checklist**, **Previous**, and **Next task** available while you scroll. **Back to checklist** restores your search, filters, and place. **Show task on screen** optionally pins the selected task’s objective and a preparation tip. Completing or skipping the task clears the note.

Open the **Hespori checklist** to see the last observed patch status, seed stock, and future attempts covered. Visit the cave to record the patch. You can enable optional reminders from the checklist.

## Confirm a requirement

1. Open **Requirements & kit** on a task card. To review checks across the route, open **Route & requirements** → **Required & missing items**, choose **Show all missing items**, then expand **Needs checking**.
2. Verify the requirement in game and tick **Confirmed available** where shown. The guide saves that confirmation for your account.
3. Open **Checked requirements** in the route overview and untick **Confirmed available** to clear a confirmation. You can also manage saved preparation checks in **Account & backups** → **Progress corrections**.

## Progress and backups

Game task IDs, completion flags, and points are checked against the achievement catalogue. Boss kills, items, and manual preparation checks help plan your next task. The reward prompt follows verified game progress. If the game catalogue changes, the guide retains saved progress while it waits for compatible game data.

Progress and your last personal bank scan stay on your computer. **Account & backups** supports exporting and restoring profiles, importing from the older HTML guide, and reviewing saved progress against the game. Task and source links open in your browser when selected.

## Report a bug

Use **Report a Bug** in the sidebar or open the [GitHub bug report template](https://github.com/Zipdude/hilt-helper/issues/new?template=bug-report.md). Include your Hilt Helper and RuneLite versions, account type, selected guide, route, and steps to reproduce the issue. Keep character names, IDs, bank data, and full logs private.
