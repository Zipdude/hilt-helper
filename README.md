# Hilt Helper

A RuneLite guide and progress tracker for Old School RuneScape (OSRS) Combat Achievements (CAs). It uses the achievements you’ve earned to suggest what to do next toward **Ghommal’s hilt 2** at 169 points (Medium) or **Ghommal’s hilt 3** at 436 points (Hard). Routes support all standard-world account types, with Ironman-friendly preparation.

## Install

Open **Plugin Hub** in RuneLite, search for **Hilt Helper**, and select **Install**. Log in on a standard world and open **Hilt Helper** from the sidebar.

## Use the guide

1. Choose **Medium** or **Hard** when the guide first opens. Use **Change guide** to switch goals using the same account progress.
2. Choose **Solo** for independent activities or **With a group** to include party activities. Switch routes under **Route & requirements**.
3. Open your bank when prompted. The guide combines banked, carried, and worn supplies. Ultimate Ironmen use inventory and equipment.
4. Open the suggested task or search the checklist by name, activity, or ID. **Requirements & kit** shows what to bring or equip. **Walkthrough & sources** has task tips and reference links.
5. Complete the task in game. The guide follows your Combat Achievement points and advances to the next unfinished objective.

Open the **Hespori checklist** to see your last observed patch status, seed supply, and future attempts covered. Visit the cave to record the patch. Enable reminders in the checklist if you want notifications.

## Filters and dropdowns

| Control | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Activity** | All activities or the tasks for a particular boss or activity. |
| **Suggested tasks** | Tasks in your current plan toward the remaining points. |
| **Selected tasks** | The guide’s core tasks for the route. |
| **Reserve tasks** | Extra objectives you can use to replace tasks in your plan. |
| **All guide tasks** | The combined list of core and reserve objectives. |
| **Required & missing items** | Gear and supplies for unfinished tasks. **Show all missing items** lists confirmed shortages under **Missing or short** and unresolved requirements under **Needs checking**. Tick **Confirmed available** on manual checks after verifying them in game. |

Open **Filters** to change the activity or task type and to hide completed tasks. **Route & requirements** compares route points, and **Blocked tasks** shows the quest, level, or kit holding up a task.

## Extended Guide Description

**Solo** starts with easier independent activities and keeps raid and party reserves out of its suggestions. **With a group** shares the early start and can add Chambers, Theatre Entry and Hueycoatl reserves after their prep checks. Theatre Entry asks for **A Night at the Theatre** as a route readiness check. The separate room tasks can award before a full clear. Open **Route & requirements** to review the point plan, preparation or change routes. Both routes use the same saved points and progress.

Select **Open next task** or click a checklist card. The task screen shows the current objective and tip, with up to four upcoming objectives below. Scroll down and choose **Open task** on any of those cards to see its details. When the current task is completed, the guide opens the next unfinished task. It continues with suggested tasks and returns to the reward overview at the selected point target. **Back to checklist**, **Previous**, and **Next task** stay visible while you scroll. Expand **Requirements & kit**, **Walkthrough & sources**, or **Task options** as needed. **Show task on screen** pins the objective and a kit note in the game view. Completing or skipping the task clears the note.

The guide shows how many remaining points are ready across the chosen route. Expand **Route & requirements** to compare unearned unlocked and locked points for Solo and Group. The numbers follow the task type, activity and search filters. Open **Required & missing items** in the same panel to choose an activity and see required kit, quantities and readiness for unfinished tasks in the filter. In **Show all missing items**, **Needs checking** lists unconfirmed requirements and **Missing or short** lists confirmed shortages. For a manual requirement, tick **Confirmed available** after checking it in game. This saves a preparation correction for your account. Select the activity in the item filter to review or untick a saved confirmation. The panel also lists outstanding quests and skill thresholds across the active route. **Review preparation** opens the next task’s checklist when its tracked checks are outstanding. **Blocked tasks** lists the first missing check for each task in the current filter. Task cards retain individual kit checks. Choose other tasks to cover the same points as you progress.

**Selected tasks** shows the guide’s core tasks for every account type. **Suggested tasks** is the current point-targeted plan, which can include reserves. Type a task name, activity, rule or ID and press Enter or **Search**. Use **Filters** for task type, activity and completion visibility. Collapse **Task list** to hide the cards. Expanded lists grow with the sidebar, and the **Checklist** shortcut and **Account & backups** stay in view underneath. Hover over the cards to scroll the list, or move outside to scroll the sidebar. **Back to checklist** retains search, filters and scroll position. **Skipped tasks & activities** collects restore controls.

Visit the cave to record the patch, then choose **Open Hespori checklist** to see its status, seed stock and how many future attempts they cover. While the crop is growing, the guide can suggest other tasks. When the growth estimate elapses, the checklist asks you to check the patch. After a kill it shows the harvest and replant steps and flags a seed shortage there when your observed stock is empty. **Hespori Adept** takes five kills, and **Hesporisn’t** needs a damaging special attack for the finishing blow. Each is worth three points. **Enable Hespori reminders** adds optional notifications while logged in. **Close Hespori checklist** returns to where you were.

Validated completions at any difficulty count toward both rewards. Hard starts at the player’s current total and can suggest unfinished Medium activities. Existing profiles retain completions, saved progress, preparation, skips, imports and route style. Hard uses the game’s current validated total when it is available. Claim guidance requires a validated total at the selected target.

Open your bank so the guide can check your owned items. Item quantities and spell recipes include inventory, equipment and bank supplies. Carried rune pouch contents count when the client can read them. The guide uses the last scan across logins and asks for a refresh if an older bank snapshot may overlap with a carried stack. On the task card, **Bring from your bank** and **Requirements & kit** tell you what to withdraw or wear. Shellbane’s worn weight is shown on the relevant attempt. Ultimate accounts use inventory and equipment. Group supplies count once withdrawn to your own inventory.

Slayer reserves use individual monster kills at existing levels, with their required protection and equipment. An assignment is not a universal requirement; do not train hours of Slayer for a one-point reserve.

Wilderness is excluded unless you explicitly opt in. The guide distinguishes solo Hardcore, Hardcore Group and Ultimate risks. Walkthroughs, task notes and original source/video links provide reference material.

## Progress, privacy and recovery

Combat Achievement completion comes only from validated game task IDs and flags. Items, kill counts, quest progress and readiness never award a completion. The collector checks all bundled IDs, names and tiers, both reward thresholds, the completion blocks and the server point sum. An incompatible catalogue pauses new automatic evidence and retains saved progress.

Account progress is stored locally under RuneLite’s plugin-data directory, separately by the unsigned account hash and standard-world profile. This is not RuneLite cloud sync. The plugin does not upload account data, fetch remote data, download code or run an updater. Clicking a source link opens its original public URL without adding account identifiers. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

Existing native profiles update in place. Optional **Account & backups** controls support a one-time import from the old HTML guide, a preview, explicit account binding, unchanged-source archives, backups and rollback. Imports are idempotent. Automatic refresh retains prior positive completions and manual floors. **Review saved vs game points** is an explicit reconciliation with a restorable archive; it never silently resets a profile.

The guide prioritizes Ironman-friendly activities and equipment. Standard-world support includes mains, regular Ironmen, Hardcore, Ultimate, Group, Hardcore Group and unranked Group. Each account uses its own progress and available supplies. The full route and hilt reward require membership.

## Progress and privacy

Account progress and the last personal bank scan stay on your computer. Use **Account & backups** to export a progress backup or review saved preparation corrections. Restore skipped objectives under **Skipped tasks & activities**. Source links open in your browser when selected.

## Report a bug

Use **Report a Bug** in the sidebar or open the [GitHub bug report template](https://github.com/Zipdude/hilt-helper/issues/new?template=bug-report.md). Include the plugin and RuneLite versions, your account type, your route, and the steps that reproduce the issue. Keep character names, IDs, bank data, and full logs private.
